01 — About

Munirah Bader
Almahmoud

"Creating spaces that are modern, elegant, and deeply comfortable — through calm colours, natural materials, and simple details."

I am Munirah Almahmoud, an interior designer who enjoys creating modern, elegant and comfortable spaces. My focus is on calm colours, natural materials and simple details to craft balanced, functional environments that make the very most of every corner.

From the initial client brief through to the final styling, I bring a disciplined eye and a genuine care for the people who will inhabit each space — believing that great design is not only beautiful, but deeply liveable.

My work spans residential, commercial, and hospitality projects, each approached with the same commitment: to translate a client's vision into spaces that feel entirely their own.
